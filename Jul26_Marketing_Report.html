Reporting note — these figures will move

Process

HubSpot deal amounts and contact lead statuses are live fields that keep changing after a month closes. Everything in this report is a snapshot as at 3 Aug 2026, and July's records are only days old.

  • Deal amounts get revised as scoping firms up — in both directions
  • Contacts are promoted to MQL and SQL well after the month they were created
  • Open deals move to Closed Won, Closed Lost or Inactive

Expect July's deal value and SQL count to rise as records mature, so read these as conservative. The durable fix is to export and freeze a month-end snapshot on the 1st of each month — that gives every future comparison a fixed reference point instead of a moving one.
